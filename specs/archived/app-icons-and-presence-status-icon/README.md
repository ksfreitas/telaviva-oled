# App Icons and Presence Status Icon

This archive preserves the implementation plan for the macOS app icons and the presence-aware menu-bar status icon. The work was a standalone fast feature, so it has no separate `spec.md` or `contracts.md`; requirements, decisions, tasks, deviations and verification are recorded together in [plan.md](plan.md).

- [Implementation plan, status and notes](plan.md)

## What shipped

- Vector sources in `assets/branding/`: the master `app-icon.svg`, the Liquid Glass layers, the Icon Composer document `AppIcon.icon`, and the menu-bar glyph masters.
- `Assets.car` and a full-size `AppIcon.icns`, generated by `tools/branding/build-icons.sh` and committed under `packaging/macos/Resources/`.
- `Info.plist`, `packaging/macos/build-app.sh` and Xcode project wiring for the app icon.
- Core field `camera.presence`, with Rust and FFI tests.
- Drawn template status icon with three states and localized accessibility labels.
- New tests: `tools/tests/test-status-item-icon.sh` and `tools/tests/test-branding-assets.sh`.

The decision record is [ADR 0009](../../../adr/0009-app-icons-liquid-glass-and-presence-status-icon.md).

## Still open

- Visual and hardware acceptance: Liquid Glass in Dock and Finder on macOS 26 and later, the `.icns` on macOS 14 and 15, and the menu-bar icon on light, dark and tinted menu bars.
- Confirmation of the artwork license.
- The Xcode-built app has not been tested.
- Interpretation of "o ícone do relógio" as the menu-bar icon.
